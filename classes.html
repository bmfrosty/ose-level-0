B/X & OSE Class Tables

Smoothified progressions — to-hit bonus and saving throws for all classes, levels 1–14

Smoothified Mode uses gradual attack bonus and saving throw progressions instead of the original 1981 B/X D&D and OSE big jumps. All classes advance every 1–2 levels. Demihuman level limits are removed — all classes reach level 14.

For official OSE Standard progressions — a restatement of 1981 B/X D&D's own tables — see the OSE SRD.
